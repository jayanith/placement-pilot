Project: PlacementPilot
Author: Jayanith Chowdhary (GitHub: jayanith)
Built for: BharatCode Build League, Round 1 (2026)
Direction: Work & automation / Everyday assistance
Model: BharatCode Qwen 3.8 27B (qwen-3.8-27b) via https://bharatcode.ai/api/model/v1/chat/completions

What it does:
PlacementPilot reads a job post and tells an Indian fresher, in seconds, whether they can
genuinely apply (experience and batch requirements, not just the portal's "Freshers" tag),
flags scam signs such as registration fees, and tailors their résumé to the role without
inventing anything. Web app (FastAPI) and CLI.

How BharatCode is used:
All reasoning (requirement extraction, eligibility verdict, résumé tailoring, cover note)
runs on qwen-3.8-27b through the OpenAI-compatible Chat Completions endpoint, with 503
retry and finish_reason:length handling as the BharatCode docs recommend.
